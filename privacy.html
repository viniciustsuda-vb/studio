Privacy policy: Studio Publisher

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Who uses this app

Studio Publisher is used only by the owner of the YouTube channels Net of Hype, Third Hypothesis and Chalk Mark, with his own Google account. It is not available to other users.

What data it accesses

With the owner's permission, the app uses the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API to:

  • upload, schedule and update the studio's own videos, thumbnails and playlists (scopes youtube.upload and youtube);
  • read the analytics of the studio's own channels (scope yt-analytics.readonly).

It does not access any other user's data, and it does not read comments, messages, contacts or any personal information.

Where the data is kept

Access tokens and analytics reports are stored only on the studio's own computer. They are never sold, shared or sent to any third party, and they are not used for advertising.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube API use is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the stored tokens on the studio's computer removes all locally kept access.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact options on the channels' YouTube "About" pages.
