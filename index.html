Studio Publisher

Studio Publisher is a private tool that a small independent studio uses to publish and measure its own YouTube channels:

  • Net of Hype (business and economics)
  • Third Hypothesis (official accident investigations)
  • Chalk Mark (real espionage cases)

The tool runs only on the studio's own computer. It uploads and schedules the studio's videos, sets their thumbnails and playlists, and reads the channels' own analytics. It is not offered to the public and is used only with the studio's own Google account.
